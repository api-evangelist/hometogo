# HomeToGo

> HomeToGo is the world's largest marketplace and search engine for vacation rentals, aggregating roughly 20 million offers from thousands of providers worldwide. Its B2B arm, HomeToGo_PRO, powers the supply side of travel with SaaS and tech-enabled services for hosts and property managers, and its Doppelgänger program lets partners integrate HomeToGo inventory via API, White Label, Travel Agency Hub, ad placement, and pop-under surfaces. HomeToGo does not publish a public, self-serve API specification; API access is granted through its partner/supply programs.

## Partner & integration
- [Partner integrations](https://www.hometogo.com/partner/): API, White Label, and widget options to embed HomeToGo vacation-rental inventory.
- [Doppelgänger affiliate/partner program](https://www.hometogo.com/doppelgaenger/): five integration types — White Label, API, Ad Placement, Pop-under, Travel Agency Hub.
